The mind of the ritual

A ritual designed to hold attention.

Tea is experienced before it is tasted: aroma arrives first, then warmth, flavour, texture and expectation. Human research has reported acute effects of tea-related caffeine, L-theanine and attention measures; small rosemary studies also make its aroma scientifically interesting.

VKOLT does not present the blend as a medicine or cognitive treatment. The opportunity is more refined: create a sensory ritual that feels distinctive, memorable and focused.

The reserve programme

Rarity should be real.

We are exploring a future reserve programme built around exceptional tea cultivars and small harvests. Research into specialty cultivars such as albino Baiye-1 and Kenya’s TRFK 306 purple tea shows how unusual plant genetics can create a visibly and chemically distinctive tea category.

These would be developed as carefully sourced limited editions or companion expressions, rather than casually altering the patented VKOLT signature formulation.

Organic by design

Earn the organic claim.

VKOLT's goal is to develop the signature blend around certified-organic raw materials and controlled processing. Under India's organic framework, a multi-ingredient product can be labelled “Certified Organic” when at least 95% of its ingredients are of certified organic origin; certification, traceability and correct labelling remain essential.

We will not place a “100% organic” claim or organic certification marks on the product simply because the recipe is plant-based. The certification and label must be earned and documented.
