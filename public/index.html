The company

Built around the leaf.

VKOLT is a tea-focused company developing distinctive products for people who care about what they drink and how it is presented. We bring together tea, botanicals, thoughtful formulation, and premium presentation.

From refined personal rituals to high-end hospitality, our ambition is simple: create tea worth remembering.

The origin

Green hills. Cool air. A slower beginning.

VKOLT starts with the idea that tea should feel connected to its natural setting — fresh, calm and quietly expressive.

The leaf

Fresh growth, caught in the light.

A brighter visual language brings the tea plant itself forward — vivid greens, open daylight and movement that feels like a breeze across the field.

The botanical detail

Texture you can almost touch.

Micro movement in the foliage, gentle focus shifts and soft highlights create a living botanical moment without turning the page into a gimmick.

The ritual

Then the leaf becomes a moment.

Warm tea, clear light and rising steam finish the journey — a human ritual grounded in the landscape that came before it.

Leaf → botanical → ritual
